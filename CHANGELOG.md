# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- **Extract with a double-click.** An archive opened from the Finder or the Dock is
  unpacked beside itself, into a folder of its own when it holds loose files, or
  opened in a window, or JustArc asks, as Settings says.
- **A window for archives.** Browse, preview with Quick Look, extract a selection,
  drag files out, add files by dropping them in any folder, delete, rename, and save
  in another format with a password, encrypted names or split volumes. Files opened
  from an archive and edited elsewhere are offered back to it.
- **Formats.** Reads ZIP, 7-Zip, RAR, TAR and its compressed forms, GZip, BZip2, XZ,
  Zstandard, CAB, ISO, CPIO, DEB, RPM, WIM, LZH, ARJ, XAR and disk images, through
  7-Zip 25.01 built into the app. Writes ZIP, 7-Zip, TAR and its compressed forms,
  GZip, BZip2, XZ and disk images, and RAR with RARLAB's tool, which Settings can
  download.
- **In the Finder.** Compress with JustArc, Extract with JustArc and Open Archive in
  JustArc in the right-click menu; a Quick Look preview that lists what is in an
  archive; a choice of default app for each kind of archive in Settings.
- **Careful extraction.** Nothing is written outside the folder chosen, links are
  checked before they are made, a cancelled extraction is undone, and files and
  folders already there are replaced, kept or merged as asked.
- **English and Russian.**

Builds are signed but not yet notarized; see the README for opening one the first
time.
